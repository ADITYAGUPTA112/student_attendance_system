menu_book Distributed MapReduce Paradigm Walkthrough (Viva Reference) expand_more
Step 1: Map

Each attendance tuple emits intermediate (key, (is_present, 1)) pairs across simulated partition workers.

Step 2: Shuffle & Sort

Pairs are partitioned and deterministically sorted by course code, session date, and learner key before reduction.

Step 3: Reduce

Reducers sum present counts and total observations per partition key to yield accurate attendance percentages.
